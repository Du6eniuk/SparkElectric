Brand exploration · Round 1

SparkElectric — logo concepts

Three directions, each with a palette sampled from the logo that carries straight into the website. Every mark is vector, holds up at favicon size, and works on light and dark backgrounds.

01

Power On

Modern · reliable · clean

The universal power symbol, with its bar redrawn as a bolt of lightning. It captures the moment the lights come back on, a feeling every Floridian knows after a storm. The crisp geometric wordmark and electric blue feel like a modern, tech-savvy company you can book online. Yellow is saved for the spark, so it always catches the eye.

02

Neon Palm

Local · bold · unforgettable

A Florida palm whose trunk is a lightning bolt, drawn like the neon signs of Miami Beach, so electricity and Florida share one symbol. The script-and-caps pairing has a retro, after-dark glow that no other electrician has. On the site, the logo and headlines can glow and flicker on like a real sign.

03

Live Wire

Bold · established · trade

An S bent from a single insulated wire, stripped back to bare copper at both ends, just like every job starts. Heavy extended capitals read from across the street on a van, yard sign or crew shirt. Copper on carbon looks premium and stands apart from the usual yellow-and-black. The most “serious contractor” of the three.
