What we do

Everything electric, done right

From a dead outlet to a full panel upgrade, one licensed team handles the diagnosis, the permits and the work.

  • Panel Upgrades

    Swap out Federal Pacific, Zinsco and fuse panels, or step up to 200-amp service for today’s loads.

  • EV Chargers

    Level 2 home chargers for any make, on a dedicated circuit with the permit and load check handled.

  • Generators

    Standby generators and transfer switches, so hurricane season doesn’t mean days in the dark.

  • Surge Protection

    Florida sees more lightning per square mile than any other state. Whole-home protection guards every appliance.

  • Lighting

    Recessed, under-cabinet and landscape lighting, designed to set the mood and trim the power bill.

  • Ceiling Fans

    New fans on fan-rated boxes, balanced and wired to wall controls. A Florida essential.

  • Pool & Spa

    Pumps, heaters, lights and bonding, wired to code by electricians who know wet locations.

  • Repairs

    Flickering lights, tripping breakers and dead outlets, traced to the source and fixed right.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you really answer 24/7?

Yes. Our emergency line is answered around the clock by our own team, not a call center. For sparking, burning smells or a total loss of power, an electrician heads out right away.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. SparkElectric is a state-certified electrical contractor with full liability and workers’ comp coverage. Every job is done or supervised by a licensed electrician.

How much does a panel upgrade cost?

It depends on your home’s service size and what the upgrade involves. We do a free on-site assessment and give you a flat-rate price, so you know the full cost before we start. Most upgrades take a single day.

Can you install my EV charger?

Yes, for any make. We check that your panel can handle the load, run a dedicated circuit, pull the permit and mount a Level 2 charger where it’s easiest for you to plug in.

Do I need a permit for electrical work?

Most electrical work beyond simple repairs needs one in Florida. We pull the permit, schedule the inspection and handle the paperwork, so you don’t have to.
